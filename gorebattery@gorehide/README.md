# GoreBattery

A low-battery alert for the Cinnamon panel. When the battery drops below a level you choose **and the computer is unplugged**, a glow appears around the edges of every screen.

## Features

- Choose which battery to monitor (laptops with more than one battery, or peripherals that report a battery).
- Alert threshold from 1% to 99%.
- The alert only triggers while the charger is **unplugged**, and disappears as soon as you plug it in.
- A thin border around the screen while the computer is unplugged, whatever the charge level (configurable color and width, independent from the glow).
- The glow and the border can each be turned off independently.
- Three glow styles: soft glow, two-color gradient and animated fire, with configurable colors.
- Live preview: while the applet settings window is open, the glow stays visible so you can see your changes.
- Configurable glow duration (or keep it until you plug the charger in) and check interval.
- Panel icon with a flame that grows as the battery gets lower.

## Notes

The glow is drawn over the whole screen but never captures the mouse or keyboard.
