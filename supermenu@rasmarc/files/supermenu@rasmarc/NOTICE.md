# SuperMenu 1.0.8 — credits and license

SuperMenu is maintained by rasmarc. It is distributed under the GNU General
Public License, version 2 or later. See `COPYING`.

The applications menu and its helpers are derived from
[`menu@cinnamon.org` in Cinnamon](https://github.com/linuxmint/cinnamon/tree/6.6.9/files/usr/share/cinnamon/applets/menu%40cinnamon.org)
by Linux Mint and the Cinnamon contributors. The bundled development template
was adapted from the Cinnamon 6.6 menu. Its source hash and template details
are recorded in `source-info.json`. Original notices in the code are retained.

SuperMenu adds a second panel control, alternative menu layouts, custom menu
editing and storage, and its own settings and translations. Its logo and
bundled icon variants were supplied by rasmarc.

The applet uses Cinnamon and XApp's installed dialogs and libraries. It reads
the CustomApplicationsMenu backup format; it does not include that applet's
code.
