# Asyntai AI Chatbot

[Asyntai](https://asyntai.com) is an AI chatbot for your website. It answers your visitors from your own content, collects leads and hands chats over to your team. This applet brings that work to your Cinnamon panel.

## What the applet does

- **New chats on the panel.** A number next to the icon shows how many chats on your website have new messages. It resets when you open the menu.
- **Notifications.** A notification shows each new chat and each new lead, with the visitor's question or email address.
- **Ask your chatbot.** Type a question in the menu and read the answer your chatbot gives your visitors. Ask a follow-up and it keeps the conversation. Copy the answer with one click.
- **Recent chats, leads and tickets.** Open a list and click a row to read the whole chat in your Asyntai dashboard.

## Setup

1. Sign in to Asyntai, open **Settings**, then **API**, and copy your API key.
2. Right-click the applet, choose **Configure**, and paste the key.

The applet checks for new chats every 5 minutes. You can change this, and turn the panel number and the notifications on or off, in the same settings.

The Asyntai API needs the Starter plan or higher.

Step-by-step guide: [Asyntai AI Chatbot for Cinnamon](https://asyntai.com/documentation/integrations/cinnamon/)

## Requirements

- Cinnamon 5.2 or later (Linux Mint 21 or later)
