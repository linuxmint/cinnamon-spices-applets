# Harald Bluetooth Manager

A minimalist Blue tooth manager applet for Linux Cinnamon

![Screenshot](screenshot.png)

## Features

- **Device Management**: Show your Blue Tooth devices in a minimalist pop-up 
- **Scanning & Pairing**: Search for available Blue Tooth Devices and Pair with them.
- **Connect / Disconnect**: Easily connect and disconnect to your Blue Tooth devices
- **Customizable appearance**: Change color of Background, buttons and text. Make it fit your theme.

## Requirements

This applet relies on standard system libraries, but ensure you have `glib` and `gtop` bindings available (installed by default on most Linux Mint installations).

## Settings

Right-click the applet on your desktop and select **Configure** to access:
- Change Opacity.
- Change Button color
- Change Text color
- Blue Tooth device color
