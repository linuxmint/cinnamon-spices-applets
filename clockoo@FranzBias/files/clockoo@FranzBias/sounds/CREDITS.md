Every sound in this folder is released under **Creative Commons 0 (CC0 1.0)**
— public domain dedication: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>.
No attribution is required, but we give it anyway, with thanks to the authors.

All files come from [Freesound](https://freesound.org). The license was checked
on each sound's page on 2026-10-23. They were taken from Freesound's
high-quality previews, then trimmed of leading silence, loudness-normalized
(−16 LUFS, EBU R128) and re-encoded as Ogg Vorbis for Clockoo.

Want your own sounds? In Clockoo's settings choose any folder: every
`.ogg`, `.oga`, `.wav`, `.mp3`, `.flac` or `.opus` file in it shows up in the
sound lists. Files in your own folder are never copied into this repository.
